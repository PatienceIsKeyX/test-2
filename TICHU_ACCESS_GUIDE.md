# How to Access and Play Tichu on dod.gr

[dod.gr](https://www.dod.gr/) is a Greek online gaming platform offering over 25 classic multiplayer games, including **Tichu** — the popular card game.

---

## Ways to Play

### 1. Web Browser (Desktop)

Visit the Tichu game page directly:

> **https://www.dod.gr/games/tichu/**

No installation needed — just open it in your browser and log in or play as a guest.

### 2. Mobile / Tablet App

Download the **dod Games** app:

- [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=gr.dod.mobile&hl=en)
- [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gr/app/dod-games/id1462442100)

After installing, navigate to the **Tichu** game from the app's game list.

---

## Account Options

You have three ways to get started:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **dod Account** | Register a free account on dod.gr for full access to all features |
| **Facebook Login** | Log in quickly using your existing Facebook account |
| **Guest / Visitor** | Explore the platform without registration (limited features) |

### Registering a New Account

1. Go to [dod.gr](https://www.dod.gr/)
2. Click the **New User** button
3. Fill in your username, email, and password
4. Confirm your email and log in
5. Navigate to **Games → Tichu** to start playing

---

## What is Tichu?

Tichu is a strategic card game for 4 players (2 teams of 2). The goal is to be the first team to reach 1000 points by getting rid of your cards and earning points through special combinations.

Key elements:
- Played with a standard 52-card deck plus 4 special cards (Tichu Dragon, Phoenix, Hound, Mahjong)
- Teams sit opposite each other
- Players can announce "Tichu" (or "Grand Tichu") to bet they'll be first to go out, earning bonus points
